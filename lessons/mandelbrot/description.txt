The Mandelbrot set with no loop over pixels: every point is iterated at once, in X_eTaL, an experimental typed array language.

1. A complex number for every pixel: one row of real parts, one column of imaginary parts, spread over the grid by a table
2. One step for every pixel at once: z squared plus c, with a mask (not an if) keeping escaped points where they are
3. Repeat the step: after a couple dozen steps, what is left is the set

Then the live page runs the same program in your browser. Click a point to see its orbit.

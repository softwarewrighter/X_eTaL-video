A heart drawn by circles turning on circles, and the program has no loop over circles.

In X_eTaL, an experimental typed array language:
1. Every speed times every moment is one table of angles, an outer product
2. Two matrix products turn the points of the curve into circles, sorted biggest first
3. One running sum along the chain gives where every circle ends, at every moment
4. Each column of that sum is the curve rebuilt from that many circles: eight circles give the heart exactly

Then the live page runs the same program in your browser on a star, a flower, or a curve you draw.

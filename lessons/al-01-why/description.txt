Array languages, episode 1. Most programming languages teach us to think one step at a time: create a variable, loop, take an element, operate, store, repeat. Array programming describes the whole computation instead. This episode compares a Rust loop with whole-array operations, traces the array family from Iverson's 1962 notation through APL\360, APL2, J, K and BQN, and introduces X_eTaL: a statically typed array language, typed in plain ASCII and drawn as notation.

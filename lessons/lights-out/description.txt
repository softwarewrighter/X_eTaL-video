Lights out on a 5 by 5 board, and the program has no loop over the lights.

In X_eTaL, an experimental typed array language:
1. A press is a plus-shaped mask, one row of a table of all 25 presses, built from two tables of distances
2. A move is the board plus that mask, mod 2: every light toggled at once
3. Pressing many squares at once is one inner product with that table
4. The solver is the same table, reduced mod 2 (Gaussian elimination): out come the presses that turn every light off

Then the live page runs the game in your browser: click a light, or ask for a hint.

Three hundred points find their clusters, and the program has no loop over points.

In X_eTaL, an experimental typed array language:
1. Every point's distance to every center is one table
2. Each point's nearest center is one reduce, the place of each row's smallest, written as rows of 0s and 1s
3. The new centers are means by group: that table turned on its side, times the points, over the counts
4. Repeat the step: from a poor start three centers crowd one blob and get stuck; from a farthest-first start each center finds its own blob

Then the live page runs the same program in your browser.

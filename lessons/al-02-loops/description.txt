Array Languages, episode 2. A loop often does three jobs at once: it transforms each element, keeps some of them, and combines what it keeps. This episode separates those jobs into whole-array operations: map (three times values), filter (a mask from a comparison, then replicate), and reduce (plus, max, min, and counting with a mask). It compares Rust iterator chains, composes the three steps in X_eTaL, and previews shape, rank, and axes, the topic of the next episode.

# tbt-2741: APL\360 on a 3D IBM 2741

Throwback Thursday #15 as a narrated video (a story: `kind: "story"`,
drawn by `remotion/src/Story.tsx`), about 5 minutes, landscape, for the
softwarewrighter channel. A'70, APL\360 printing at an IBM 2741's own
pace on green-bar paper, in 3D on the 2741 model of console-3D, on the
way to a virtual computer history museum.

Sources, in this order: the TBT #15 post (software-wrighter-lab blog,
2026-11-19), sw-apl and console-3D, earlier TBT posts (#1, #11, #12,
#14). The narration says only what those say.

What it is made from, none of it copied into git:

- the A'70 page, recorded with its key, space and return sounds
  (`just capture tbt-2741`)
- console-3D's renders (`../console-3D/images/`) and the post's
  pictures (`../../software-wrighter-lab/blog/assets/images/posts/`)
- the reused closing clips, `99b-epilog.mp4` and
  `99c-epilog-ext-swing9.mp4` (muted), in
  `../video-publishing/reference/epilog/`
- the music, `~/Downloads/break-it-down.wav`: its first 5 s under the
  title, its last 7 s under the end

```bash
just voice tbt-2741 && just capture tbt-2741 && just render tbt-2741
just review tbt-2741    # http://127.0.0.1:8427/X_eTaL-video/review/tbt-2741/
just youtube tbt-2741   # out/tbt-2741-youtube/
```

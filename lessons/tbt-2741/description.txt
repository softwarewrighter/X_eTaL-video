Throwback Thursday #15. A'70, my APL\360 demo, now prints at an IBM 2741's own pace, a character at a time, on green-bar fan-fold paper, with the terminal's sounds, and in 3D on my model of the 2741 from console-3D: the carrier moves to each column and the type element tilts and turns to each character.

My first program, a horse race in APL, was typed on an IBM 2741 in 1972. The 2741 is one of nine models in console-3D so far, part of my ongoing work toward a virtual computer history museum.

A video about an array language, scheduled by that same language. No timeline editor, no keyframes: only data and arrays.

How these X_eTaL videos are made:
1. A lesson is data: each scene is a heading, a few lines, a narration and a length in seconds, all in one file
2. X_eTaL computes the schedule: seconds become frames, and where each scene starts is one running sum
3. Every frame knows its place: its scene, how far into the scene it is and its progress, all frames computed at once as arrays
4. Motion is arithmetic: each line's fade-in is a column of one table, and the easing curves are arrays too
5. A renderer (Remotion) draws one picture per frame from those numbers and computes no timing of its own

The numbers on screen are the video's own schedule, live, frame by frame.

A sudoku solved with no loop over cells: the rules are a table, the candidates a mask, and a move a mask added to the grid.

In X_eTaL, an experimental typed array language:
1. The rules are a table: 27 units (9 rows, 9 columns, 9 boxes), one select and one sum count every digit in every unit
2. Every cell's candidates at once: a digit is open where none of the cell's three units holds it
3. A move is a mask: every single found at once and added to the grid in one step
4. Round after round until nothing changes: five rounds solve Wikipedia's example

Then the live page plays the game at a terminal and runs the solver as a notebook, in your browser.

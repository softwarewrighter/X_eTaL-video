One head of attention, the heart of a transformer, and the program has no loop over words.

In X_eTaL, an experimental typed array language:
1. Every word's query and key are one matrix product each
2. Rows meet columns: every query against every key in one product with a transpose, the whole table of scores
3. A softmax by row turns the scores into weights that add up to 1
4. Each word's output is the weighted mix of the values: "tired" and "it" take on the animal's meaning

Then the live page runs the same program in your browser, and "wide" finds the street.

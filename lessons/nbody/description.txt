Three bodies chase each other around a figure eight, and the program has no loop over pairs.

In X_eTaL, an experimental typed array language:
1. Every pair's displacement is one table, stacked into a cube
2. Every pair's pull is one expression, the inverse square law for all pairs at once
3. Every body's acceleration is one reduce
4. Kick, drift, kick, repeated: the orbit

Then the live page runs the same program in your browser with fifty bodies.

Conway's Game of Life, the whole rule, in a single line of X_eTaL, an experimental typed array language, shown on the live demo page itself: the published life-microscope page, recorded while a script clicks through it. Read the rule right to left:

1. Rotate the board by -1, 0 and 1 along both axes: nine shifted copies, one for each neighbor and one for the cell itself
2. Add the nine copies: every cell now holds itself plus its eight neighbors
3. Compare: a cell is alive next when the sum is 3, or when it is alive and the sum is 4

No loops over cells, no neighbor bookkeeping. Then it runs: the R-pentomino on a 16 by 16 board that wraps around, every generation the same single line.
